About Angora

One consultancy.
One industry. One standard.

Angora works exclusively with ambulance companies. Accuracy first, then efficiency. Everything else follows from that.

What we do

AI enablement for ambulance operators.

We find the repetitive work eating your team's week and build AI workflows to handle it, inside the tools you already own. No new software. No rip-and-replace. Patient data handled properly: a signed BAA before anything flows, covered tools only, and your data never trains models.

Most of the work is unglamorous on purpose: claim-status checks, schedule drafts, recurring reports, credential tracking. The admin load nobody sees and everybody feels.

We do it one way: accuracy first, then efficiency. A workflow that is fast but wrong is not a shortcut. It is a liability with better marketing.

How we work

Four rules. No exceptions.

01

Accuracy first

Every workflow gets a human verification gate before its output counts. In EMS, a confident wrong answer is worse than a slow right one.

02

Inside your tools

We build in what you already pay for. You keep control, you keep portability, and nothing breaks when a vendor changes its roadmap.

03

Meet people where they are

Some of your staff will love AI. Some will feel threatened by it. We build with both groups, not around them. Adoption is the product.

04

Patient data, handled properly

A signed BAA before any patient information flows. Only HIPAA-eligible tools with BAA coverage touch it. Your data never trains models, and every clinical-adjacent output gets a human check.

Why ambulance
