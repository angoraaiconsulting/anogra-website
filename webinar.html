What you will learn

Thirty minutes.
Three things that matter.

Part 1

The Curse of the Curve

A simple question gets a good answer from AI in five minutes. Turning that into work that keeps working takes sustained effort most employees cannot spare from their day jobs. That gap is why AI enablement flatlines, and why it is not a tool problem.

Part 2

The Margin Map method

How I find the hours in an ambulance operation: inventory every repetitive workflow, score each on hours returned and confidence it will work, build in order of highest return and lowest risk. Pick the work first. The tool is the last decision.

Part 3

Where to be careful

Dispatch decisions, the patient care record, clinical judgment, patient data. Four areas where AI needs a human in the loop, and what to watch out for if you go there. Caution is what makes everything else safe to build.
