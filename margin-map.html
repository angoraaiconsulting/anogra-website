What Map contains

What Map contains

Before a single workflow is automated: your repetitive workflows inventoried and scored, your tech stack mapped, your BAA and AI policy drafted, your build order set.

Readiness and inventory

Repetitive workflows in your operation, inventoried in plain language and scored on estimated hours and confidence they will work.

Tech stack map

What you own, what it can connect to, and what it cannot. No new platforms assumed. I build in the platforms you may already use. If new licenses are needed, the Map confirms what they cost. Licenses are paid to the vendor directly, not included in Angora fees.

Governance drafts

BAA framework and AI policy drafts, labeled for your counsel's review. Diligence support, not legal advice. Your counsel makes the final calls.

Sequenced roadmap

What to build, in what order, and what not to build. Highest estimated hours and lowest risk go first. Map ranks workflows by estimated hours and lowest risk first, and names what not to build.
