Readiness and inventory

Readiness and inventory

Repetitive workflows in the operation, inventoried in plain language and scored on estimated hours and confidence they will work.

Hand-pulled reporting, BUILD FIRST

Volume, response-time, and financial figures pulled by hand from three systems and rebuilt in a spreadsheet every month. 310 hrs/yr. Confidence: High.

Billing follow-ups

Denied and pending claims checked against payer portals, drafts queued for human review. 210 hrs/yr. Confidence: High.

Crew scheduling

Draft schedule from availability and cert requirements. Supervisor approves before posting. 150 hrs/yr. Confidence: High.

Onboarding paperwork

New-hire packets assembled from the roster and cert list. HR reviews before anything is filed. 120 hrs/yr. Confidence: Med.

Compliance tracking

License, cert, and vehicle inspection dates tracked in a shared sheet. Reminders drafted for a supervisor to send. 100 hrs/yr. Confidence: Med.

890 hrs/yr across the five scored workflows. Sample figures, not a promise.

Crew clinical decisions, HANDLE WITH CARE: Patient care judgment stays with licensed clinicians. Flagged for caution, not scored.

Tech stack map

Tech stack map

What the operation owns, what each tool can connect to, and what it cannot. No new platforms assumed.

Connects

  • ePCR system to billing system, WEEKLY EXPORT: Run data flows to billing on a weekly export. One way. No API.
  • Billing system to accounting, MONTHLY POST: Claim and payment figures posted to accounting once a month.
  • Dispatch/CAD to spreadsheets, BY HAND: Call data downloaded by hand and rebuilt in spreadsheets for reporting.

Does not connect

  • ePCR system to office suite, NO API: Reports export out of the ePCR. Nothing flows back in.
  • Dispatch/CAD to billing, MANUAL: Trip data re-entered into billing by hand.
